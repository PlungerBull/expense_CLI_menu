T1 · Post-write warning — the local copy didn’t refresh
After a successful write, if the post-write sync fails, today you get a success toast over a silently-stale replica. This adds a warning toast (severity=warning, amber). The successful case (left) is unchanged; the new warning (right) only appears when the refresh fails.
