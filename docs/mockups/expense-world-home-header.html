How "owed" is computed It's the net of your people balances (each person's balance is signed: positive = they owe you, negative = you owe them). Net > 0 → owed to you in green · net < 0 → you owe in red · net = 0 or no people → the whole segment (and its ·) is dropped. Green/red matches how balances are already sign-colored everywhere else (theme AMOUNT_RULE). One honest caveat A net headline can read owed to you 240 even if you owe Ana 180 and Beto owes you 420 — the net is right, the detail isn't shown here. That's fine for a glance line; the full per-person breakdown already lives in Outstanding Amounts. Say the word if you'd rather the slot show gross "owed to you" only and ignore what you owe. The other two stats, briefly net flips green/red by its own sign (a spendy month shows net −320 in red); spent is always the month's outflow magnitude in red. Both from dashboard.totals, home currency. Currency Amounts are bare home-currency numbers (via format_cents) — no symbol, no code — matching how money renders everywhere else in the app. Decided 2026-07-10; "no indicator" chosen over a symbol map or an ISO-code suffix.
