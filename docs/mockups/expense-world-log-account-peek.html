LOG bar — the account peek

The ask, 2026-08-29: once the line you are typing names a bank account, the lower part of the LOG screen shows that account’s transactions, so you can see where you stand there while you are still typing.

Nothing new is needed from the engine. GET /transactions?account_id=… already exists and the Transactions screen already calls it. One debounced request per account you name, cached for the session.

v2 — your three corrections, 2026-08-29

  • The account is the bar’s, never a staged row’s. The peek follows the $token in the line you are typing; the staged rows below never vote, not even when they all name the same account. The one case this leaves open is pane 5.
  • Not five rows. Variant A takes every free row; variant B is a fixed window of twelve (say a number if you want a different one).
  • No balance anywhere. Gone from the card title, and the whole “projected balance after the batch” question is gone with it. The card names the account and counts the rows, nothing else.

Decided without asking, say if wrong

  • It appears the moment the $token resolves, mid-line, and disappears the moment it stops resolving (pane 7) — it is not something you switch on.
  • It follows the picker while you disambiguate (pane 6).
  • The peek is read-only. No cursor, no ↵, no editing — ↑↓ keep belonging to the staged list. It is a window, not a table you work in.
  • Columns: date, title, amount, category, tags. No account column (constant here), no description (no width for it).

Q · the two picks

1 — A elastic, or B a fixed twelve?

A is at its best exactly when you are asking the question — first line, empty screen, twelve or more rows of history — and at its worst when you have a long batch staged, which is also when you are least likely to be reading it. B never moves, so your eye learns one spot, at the cost of twelve rows the staging list cannot have even when it wants them. A unless you tell me otherwise.

2 — pane 5: when ↵ clears the bar, does the peek i stay on the account you just named, or ii vanish until you name one again?

i is what makes it useful while you type a run of lines for one account — the peek is still there between lines instead of blinking off at every ↵. ii is the strict reading of “the active account”: no account in the bar, no peek. i unless you tell me otherwise.
