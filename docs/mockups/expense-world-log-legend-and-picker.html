LOG bar — legend, picker, tags by category

Three things you raised on 2026-08-29, in the order you raised them. Two of them are bugs I could reproduce against your real ledger; the third needs something the engine does not have yet.

What I found before drawing anything

  • The legend exists already — it is just the wrong shape. The grammar line shows only while the bar is empty, so it disappears the moment you type. Three shapes below. Notes are //, to the end of the line, and they are saved as the row’s description.
  • The picker fails in exactly two ways, and neither is the one you might expect — narrowing does work ($BCP S correctly cuts six accounts to two). What fails: a bare sigil shows nothing at all, and an accent you did not type hides the tag (#banos finds no BAÑOS).
  • Tags-by-category has no source of truth. The engine has no link between a hashtag and a category — tags are a flat list, joined only to transactions. The only thing that could answer “tags used with @SALUD” is your own history, and the engine is the only place that can read all of it. See the last section.

3 · Only the tags that belong to the category

The engine cannot answer this today. A hashtag is a flat, global label joined to transactions; there is no category link on it, and nothing in the API returns “tags used with category X”. The only place that knowledge exists is your history — every @SALUD row you ever wrote — and reading all of it is the engine’s job, not a client’s: the CLI would have to page through thousands of transactions and guess from a window of them, which is both slow and wrong, and the web and iOS clients would each have to guess the same way.

So this is an engine ask: something like GET /categories/{id}/hashtags — the tags that category has been used with, most-used first. Small, read-only, and every client gets it for free. Below is what the CLI would do with it, so the ask can go out with a picture attached. Neither is buildable until the endpoint exists.

D · tags the category has been used with, first — the rest still there

Q · what I need from you

1 — the legend: A fixed line, B key block, or C live checklist?

C is A plus one idea: the same single row, but each key is lit while the line has that token. It costs the same one row and tells you what is still missing without a second sentence. B is the only one that spells out what // and the date words actually are — but it costs four rows, and those rows come straight out of the account peek. C, and the full table above lives in the ? help card where there is room for it.

2 — the two picker fixes: both, as drawn?

They are small and independent. The bare sigil is a one-line parser change; the accent fold is one normalisation in the matcher, applied to both sides. Neither touches the ranking rules. Say the word and both go in.

3 — tags by category: raise the engine ask, and which shape — D ranked or E filtered?

D keeps every tag reachable and still puts the four you actually use at the top — the gain you are after with none of the wall. E is what you asked for literally, and it is the better one if your tagging is disciplined enough that a new pairing is genuinely rare. I would send the ask with D, and it is trivial to turn D into E later — the data is the same, only the cut changes.
