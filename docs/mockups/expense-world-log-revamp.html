Log a transaction — revamp options

Drawn 2026-08-20. All five are honest Textual: every colour below is an ANSI slot (default text, dim, blue accent, red/green for sign) plus reverse video — nothing here needs a hex, and each one renders the same on a light terminal.

A · B · C are layouts — pick one. D is the quick-add parser (todo item 1) drawn against this screen. E is an add-on that bolts onto whichever layout wins.

E · recents — an add-on, not a layout

Bolt onto any of A–D. On open, your last few transactions are numbered; a digit prefills every field but the amount.

  a number prefills everything but the amount · or just start typing

Reads: transactions list, limit 5. Repeat-logging drops to three keystrokes.

Pick a letter for the layout (A, B or C — or "A+B", they compose), and say yes/no to E separately. D can wait for its own session, or ride along. Nothing gets built until you pick.
