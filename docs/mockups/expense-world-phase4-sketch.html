expense world — phase 4 sketch · money totals that can't be converted · decided 2026-08-16
Everything a user sees change in this phase. Why: on 2026-08-05 the engine stopped storing an exchange rate on each transaction and started converting when you read. Two things follow. First, a total that mixes accounts in different currencies now exists only in your home currency — the old "raw sum across currencies" number was $15 + S/25 = 40 of nothing, and it is gone. Second, if any transaction in a group falls on a date with no exchange rate, the engine refuses to give you a half-total: it returns nothing at all, plus a count of how many transactions it couldn't price. Today this repo has no idea that count exists.

Broken today: expense dashboard shows (null) for the spend of every single category — that column is its only amount — and the same on the TUI's Outstanding Amounts screen. Every Totals block prints (null) in its left half. --include-archived prints two panels that can never contain anything again. And a month that can't be priced is indistinguishable from a month where you spent nothing.

Decided 2026-08-16: C — an unpriceable figure reads 3 unrated · F — the one surviving amount column is called Home · J — the home header hides every figure and shows only the error. Plus a new rule: a category or hashtag with nothing spent is not drawn at all, in all three places these tables appear.

What does not change: every per-transaction amount (a transaction belongs to one account, so it has one currency and nothing to convert); your account and people balances, in both the native currency and the home equivalent; the accounts, categories, hashtags, transactions, inbox and reconciliation screens; and --json everywhere, which still hands you the engine's reply untouched — including the categories the tables now hide.

One consequence of hiding empty rows: the dashboard stops doubling as "the list of all my categories". expense categories list is the place that still shows every one.

Not in this phase: the engine also started returning an archived people list beside archived accounts — that is Phase 6, not an oversight here. The live run against the real engine that declares the whole surface working again is Phase 5.
decided 2026-08-16 · 1 of 3 — how "we couldn't convert this" reads
The engine says: I have no total for you, and 3 transactions are the reason. That is not zero and not blank — and it must not look like the dim — the month grid already uses for "nothing happened here". Each option is drawn twice: on the dashboard's category table, and on the range grid where it has to sit next to that existing —. The count is the actionable half — it tells you how much of the month is unpriced, and roughly how much work fixing it is.
A — n/a (3)
option
Name Spent ---------------- ------- Food -500.00 Travel n/a (3) Category 2025-12 2026-01 Food — n/a (3) everything in one cell, nothing to look up, and you know which row. 7 chars — exactly as wide as -500.00 already is, so it costs no width anywhere.
reads as "not available (3 of them)"
B — a mark, and one line underneath
option
Name Spent ---------------- ------- Food -500.00 Travel ? ? = no exchange rate (3 tx) Category 2025-12 2026-01 Food — ? ? = no exchange rate (5 tx) the cell is one character — but the column is still as wide as -500.00, so this buys no width. What it buys is a quieter table.
costs a line under every table, and the count no longer says which row
C — say it in words
chosen
Name Home ---------------- --------- Food -500.00 Travel 3 unrated Category 2025-12 2026-01 Food — 3 unrated nothing to learn, and the count is the first thing you read. 9 chars — 2 wider than the amounts in the category tables; free in the month grid, which is already 9 for -2,000.00.
clearest cold read; the only option that widens a table, by 2 characters

decided 2026-08-16 · 3 of 3 — the home header, when a figure is incomplete
Two different silences on the top line. net and spent vanish entirely when they can't be priced. owed to you is worse: it stays, looking perfectly normal, with the unpriceable people quietly left out of the sum. This header is one line pinned to the right of the wordmark, so whatever it says has to be short.

Picked 2026-08-16: C + F + J. An unpriceable figure says 3 unrated in the cell, the one surviving amount column is called Home everywhere, and the home header replaces every figure with 4 UNRATED until the rates are fixed. Option I ("leave owed alone") was rejected outright: the owed figure understating in silence is exactly the thing worth fixing.
