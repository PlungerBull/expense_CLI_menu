expense world — phase 5 sketch · the check that says the CLI works again · 2026-08-16
Phases 1–4 repaired this app blind. The fast test suite does not talk to the engine — it talks to a written-down imitation of the engine, and it has been green the entire time. Green there means the app agrees with itself. Nobody has checked it against the real thing since the rework began. Phase 5 is that check, and nothing in this repo can honestly be called working until it passes.

What went wrong when I looked: the backlog expected one broken live test. There are three. Worse, the live tests were never isolated: three of the five sign in as you, with your real saved credential, and write into your real ledger — and two of them still aim at the Render server that was switched off on 2026-07-30. The tests that were supposed to be the safety net are the least safe thing here.

Decided 2026-08-16: the verification runs against the spare database you already have, through a second throwaway engine, so your real records are never touched · all three broken files get repaired · the dead cloud address is replaced with your own machine · a re-runnable checker stays behind to catch the next engine change · C — the run refuses to start rather than write to your real ledger by accident.

4 · decided 2026-08-16 — what the run says before it writes anything
Everything above is settled. This is the one genuinely new thing a person sees, and it exists because of a sharp edge in what you picked: the default target is now your own machine on port 8000 — which is your real ledger. That default is right (the alternative was a dead server), but it means a forgotten setting sends a writing test at your real records silently. So the run announces its target first. Three shapes below. They differ in how loud they are, and in whether they can stop you.

Settled, for confirmation: the verification runs against expense_world_test through a second engine on port 8001, set up by a script living beside the one that already builds that database, with your exchange rates copied in · all three broken live tests are repaired, and the two that aim at the dead Render server are pointed at your machine · a re-runnable drift checker stays in the repo · five test fixtures stop claiming fields the engine deleted.

Picked 2026-08-16: C. The contract suite refuses to start when it would write to your real ledger, and points at the practice database instead; EXPENSE_ALLOW_REAL_LEDGER=1 is the way to say you meant it. A and B were rejected for the same reason: both only help someone who happens to be reading the output, and the moment this matters is the moment nobody is watching.
