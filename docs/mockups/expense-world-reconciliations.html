One browse screen, two focus modes. Account selector on top, that account's batches below (newest first). ↑↓ in account mode swaps the account + reloads the list; enter drops focus into the batch list; esc pops back up.
All accounts reachable from the one selector — no separate accounts page. “n” creates for whichever account is highlighted, so the create form never asks for account.
Working screen = the batch. Open draft → a checklist of that account's unassigned + already-in-this-batch transactions. Checked = in the batch; space toggles (sets/clears reconciliation_id). Transactions in other batches don't appear — a transaction lives in one batch only.
Completed = read-only. Opening a closed batch shows begin/end balances + the transactions inside, no toggling. “r” revert unlocks it (and the locked transaction fields) back to draft.
complete needs ≥1 transaction (engine 422 otherwise) and locks amount/account/title/date on them. delete is draft-only and just detaches the transactions (sets reconciliation_id back to null) — it never deletes transactions.
No “difference” meter. The engine doesn't compute one or gate completion on a balance — you decide when the batch matches your statement, then complete.
How batches link (the chain). Per account, ordered by sort_order (shown oldest→newest). A chained batch takes its begin from the row above's end (“chained ← March 2026”); a manual batch anchors the chain. ctrl+↑/↓ moves a batch in the chain (bulk reorder endpoint; chained begins recompute) — so out-of-order creates are fixed by moving, not just by manual begins.
Transaction rows show detail. Title · amount · date on top; category · #hashtags · note on a dim line below — same in draft (with checkbox) and completed (read-only).
